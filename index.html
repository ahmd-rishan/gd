Clear Answers

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Direct factual answers structured for students and search engine query understanding.

What is GD Academy? ▼

GD Academy is a practical digital skills academy based in Manjeri, Malappuram, offering hands-on training in Digital Marketing, Graphic Design, Video Editing, and WordPress web development.

Who can join GD Academy courses? ▼

Students after Plus Two, college students, job seekers, career switchers, working professionals, and business owners looking to build digital skills can enrol. No prior technical experience is required.

What is taught in the Digital Marketing course? ▼

The Digital Marketing curriculum covers Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram), Google Ads (PPC), Content Marketing, Email Marketing, Web Analytics, and WordPress site creation.

Does GD Academy offer internship and placement support? ▼

Yes, GD Academy offers 4-month agency internships for eligible diploma students along with placement support including resume polishing, portfolio creation, and mock interviews.
